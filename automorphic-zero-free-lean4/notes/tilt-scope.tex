\paragraph{Formal scope of the tilted response.}
For a finite spectral measure $\mu$ with cosine transform $h$, the
module \texttt{TiltedWindow} uses the sum of the two positive
pushforwards under $z\mapsto y\pm Wz$. Its cosine transform is
$2\cos(yx)h(Wx)$. The Poisson--Laplace identity proves that
$\int_0^\infty e^{-vx}\cos(yx)h(Wx)\,dx\ge0$ for $v>0$.
Dominated convergence extends the result to $v=0$ for an integrable
modulated window. The formalization cancels the two exponentials in
the tilted kernel to obtain $v=w-a$, verifies the boundary
integrability for the concrete quartic, and proves that a strict
trial gain excludes every width $w\le W$ under the stated disk
comparison. The signed comparison that produces the arithmetic
inequality is a separate analytic input.
